Observe its status and JSON result with kubectl get job and kubectl logs.
Successful completion installs the shared foundation and the selected deployment agent.
Control-plane enrollment and application installation are separate agent operations.

This is an initial-installation chart. It rejects Helm upgrades. Do not use Helm
rollback or reinstall to repair a retained operation. Recover the original Job
and its durable journal with the original settings and release.

Helm uninstall retains the Job, input records and installation grants for recovery;
it does not cancel the operation or revoke its authority. The installer revokes its
temporary grant on completion or bounded cleanup. If no Pod ever starts, or cleanup
cannot reach Kubernetes, an administrator must recover the original operation.
